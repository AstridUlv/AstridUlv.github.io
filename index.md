I am a PhD Fellow at the Department of Strategy and Innovation, Copenhagen Business School. My research centers on how organizational structures shape participation in knowledge-intensive work, with a particular focus on knowledge diffusion and gender inequality. 

I hold an MA from Copenhagen Business School and have been a visiting PhD fellow at the House of Innovation, Stockholm School of Economics, and at Bocconi University's Department of Management and Technology.

My job market paper "Mobility Friction and Gender Disparities in Expert Advisory Work: Evidence from the U.S. Congress" (with Y. Sofer) documents geographic mobility constraints as a consequential supply-side barrier to women's participation in high-stakes expert advising, and one that is amenable to organizational design.
