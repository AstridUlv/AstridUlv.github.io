My primary teaching interests lie in Innovation and Quantitative Methods. In innovation, I am prepared to teach courses spanning the Economics of Innovation, Innovation Strategy, and the Management of Technology, drawing directly on my research on knowledge production, scientific expertise, gender, and innovation. In quantitative methods, my experience designing and delivering live coding workshops in applied econometrics has prepared me to teach causal inference and research design at both the doctoral and master's level.
